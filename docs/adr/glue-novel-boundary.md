# The boundary of glue and novel work

Record 22 of the register. The index of the register sits in docs/decisions.md.

22. The boundary of glue and novel work. Made: the owner, 2026-11-11, issue 350, decision D1 of round 1, transcribed by issue 354. Context: the owner ruled on 2026-11-11 that an authorship scheme replaces the comprehension budget of Stance item 10 of docs/design.md. The scheme needs a boundary between the work that an agent writes and the work that the owner writes. Decision: glue is wiring between existing tools that has a pattern to copy, in this repository or upstream. Nix modules are glue, and new service containers are glue, because the repository holds a pattern for them. Novel is work without a reference path: new algorithms in the Go module, the security spine, and new design decisions. Alternatives that lost: the comprehension budget, the weekly budget of the old Stance item 10. It lost because the hand-written work itself is the practice, and no separate cadence is set (issue 350, D3). A separate policy file for the scheme, because repository-wide criteria live in the Stance of docs/design.md and standing decisions live in this register. Consequences: an agent writes glue, and the owner writes novel work by hand under the Socratic contract of record 23. The scheme governs the fixes of campaign A of issue 350, and it binds this repository only. A produced project declares its own regime (issue 350, L7). Stance item 10 of docs/design.md states the scheme. Status: accepted. Reopens: none named.
