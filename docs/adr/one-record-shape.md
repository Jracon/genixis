24. One record shape for workaround and register records. Made: the owner, 2026-11-13, issue 350, decision D14 of the rulings round, transcribed by issue 354. Context: the review of the documentation campaign left three findings on records (issue 350, P1, P3, P5). The rule of the workaround record and the record taxonomy disagreed (P1). The register records carried a reason line inconsistently, against the adr craft (P5). The why sections of three topic files named no lost options and no reopen events (P3). Decision: workaround records and register records carry the same fields, the adr form: context, decision, options that lost, consequences, status, and reopen trigger. A workaround record keeps four statuses: remove, solve, keep as a stated design, and deferred. A deferred record states its cause, its trigger, and the event that ends the deferral. The deferred status takes the place of the pending verdict of record 18. Alternatives that lost: the two shapes as they were, because the disagreement between the rule and the record taxonomy was the defect that P1 named. A rewrite of the old register records into the shape, because the register is append-only and the old records stay (issue 350, P5). Consequences: the Workaround Discipline of docs/design.md names the shared fields and the four statuses. Each new register record carries the shape, and the earlier records stay as they are (issue 350, P5). The why sections of docs/project.md, docs/cli.md, and docs/terminal-aesthetic.md move to the shape (issue 350, P3). Their repository-wide decisions move to this register, and a pointer stays behind in each section. The workaround records that hold a pending verdict keep it until a change brings each of them to the deferred status. Status: accepted. Reopens: none named.
