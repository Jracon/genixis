Decision: the words of the owner given in a window session ride the forge entity. The brief of a windowed leg obliges the leg to post the words that the owner types into the session word for word as a comment on its entity in the same run. The first line of that comment is the provenance mark, and a review verifies the quoted words of the owner against the comment. No leg reads a transcript of the harness, in any scope. The cost telemetry of the campaign close record ends with the ruling, and the record derives from the forge alone. No new label and no new event carries the duty. The brief of the windowed leg carries it, and the needs-owner continuation and the loop commands cover each correction flow. Alternatives that lost: a read of all transcripts for each leg, because the projects directory crosses issues and projects, a transcript holds tool output that can hold secret values and private words of the owner, and a reviewer that reads the narration of the worker loses its independence. A transcript read scoped to the entity of the leg, because it needs a per-leg path rule, the allow rules match the start of a command, and the words would stay in a machine-local file that rotates. No change, because each quoted ruling of a window session blocks its review, and the block recurs. Consequences: the factory skill carries the route in its windowed-leg paragraph, states the transcript prohibition, and names no cost telemetry in the campaign close record. The window-route section of the dispatcher reference names the route. A ruling quoted from a window session verifies against the comment of its windowed leg, and no review needs a transcript. Status: accepted. Reopens: the owner names a second channel for the words of a window session, or allows a transcript read in a named scope.
