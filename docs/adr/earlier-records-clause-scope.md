# The scope of the earlier-records clause of record 24

Record 25 of the register. The index of the register sits in docs/decisions.md.

25. The scope of the earlier-records clause of record 24. Made: 2026-11-13, issue 358. Context: the consequences of record 24 say that each new register record carries the fields of the adr form, and that the earlier records stay as they are (issue 350, P5). The clause about the earlier records sits in a sentence about register records, and it reads two ways: it covers the earlier register records only, or it covers the earlier records of both kinds. No ruling covers the workaround records that already hold a verdict. Issue 358 names five of them: the mirror fallback and the hand-edited hosts entry of docs/runbook.md, the ssh route record and the rclone-webdav record of docs/services.md, and the local permission rules record of docs/forge-hook.md. Decision: the clause covers the earlier register records only. The sentence and its source, finding P5 of issue 350, speak about register records. The register alone holds the append-only law that keeps the old records in place (Law of the Register, item 1). The five records that issue 358 names carry the fields of the adr form from this change, and their verdicts stand as their statuses. Alternatives that lost: the reading that the clause covers both kinds, because it leaves the five records with no disposition and rebuilds the disagreement of finding P1 of issue 350. A stated rule that keeps the five records in their old form, because the Workaround Discipline names the fields of each entry, and five standing entries without the fields contradict the discipline. Consequences: the five records carry the fields in their topic files. The workaround records that hold a pending verdict keep it under record 24. The earlier register records stay as they are (issue 350, P5). Status: accepted. Reopens: none named.
