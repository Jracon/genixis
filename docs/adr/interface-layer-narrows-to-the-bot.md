# The interface layer narrows to the bot

A record of the register, made 2026-11-19. The index of the register sits in docs/decisions.md.

Made: the owner, 2026-11-14, issue 393, the dispositions of the campaign. Context: phase 7 of issue 74 planned the interface layer as the bot, the weekly digest, the scheduled deep review, and the monthly compaction leg, with the hive memory of phase 6 as their substrate. The stage 0 verdict of issue 166 reshaped the phase: the bot is an interface only, and the digest, the deep review, and the compaction kept their text. The digest and the compaction then collided with decision D13 of issue 350, because the loop creates no new standing state and every record derives from the forge, so both lost their driver. Decision: the interface layer builds the bot alone. The weekly digest, the scheduled deep review, and the monthly compaction leg are cut, and the hive memory is cut with them. Documentation Law item 5 of docs/design.md becomes tree-only: the tree is the only home of project knowledge, and no hive memory is built. The deep review lives on as the act of the owner at the C7 dogfood exit of issue 350, with no schedule and no artifact. Alternatives that lost: the layer as written, because the digest and the compaction collide with D13 of issue 350 and lost their driver; deferral with triggers, because the substrate of the bot runs and a cut with a record beats a deferral with no trigger. Consequences: natural language reaches the loop through the bot and through the forge commands alone (docs/design.md, Stance item 8), and the observability surface stays the board, the agent view, and the derived record (docs/services.md, issue 350, L10). The knowledge of the project stays in the tree and on the forge (docs/adr/agent-knowledge-repo-and-forge.md). Status: accepted. Reopens: an owner need for a scheduled digest, a review schedule, or a compaction of learning notes.
