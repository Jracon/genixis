# The merge policy of the loop

Record 26 of the register. The index of the register sits in docs/decisions.md.

26. The merge policy of the loop. Made: 2026-11-13, issue 356, L6; the owner took D12 on 2026-11-11 in the lean-loop round of issue 350. Context: record 17 ended every merge by an agent, with the reopen event of a produced project that asks an agent to merge into its composition branch, and decision D12 of issue 350 sets the merge delegation of the lean loop. Decision: the owner merges into main and the other protected branches. Below the protected branches, delegation is wide open: the loop contract of a brief may delegate a merge to a worker leg. This record takes the place of record 17, which allowed no merge by an agent, while the global agent rules keep no exception of their own. The forge hook stays the wired bound: it denies a merge into a trunk branch, allows one squash merge into a branch that ends in /composition, and asks about every other merge (the forgejo skill). Alternatives that lost: the blanket no of record 17, because a composition flow below protected branches needs a delegated merge; a hook allow of every merge below the protected branches, because a merge that no brief delegated stays with the owner. Consequences: the factory skill states the policy, a delegated merge is an act of a worker leg under its brief, and a reviewer leg never merges. Status: accepted. Reopens: a merge attempt of a leg that no brief delegated.
