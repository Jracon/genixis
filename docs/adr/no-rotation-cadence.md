# No rotation cadence for tokens and keys

A record of the register, made 2026-11-19. The index of the register sits in docs/decisions.md.

Made: the owner, 2026-11-19, issue 373, stated in the window session of the worker leg as the answer to its park comment of 2026-11-18. The closing comment of the issue quotes the statement word for word. Context: pass A7 of campaign A (issue 366, verdict item one) found that no rule of docs/secrets.md names when a token or a key rotates. The inventory of agenix-rules.nix holds 24 age files across the two key channels, and its oldest credentials are unchanged since 2025-03-23: the mealie environment and the gamevault database, then the tailscale client secret since 2025-04-14. A rotation of a credential is a manual step, and it rides a swap of key material across the machines that hold the private halves of its channel. The repository holds no mechanism that makes that swap easy. Decision: no rotation cadence binds the tokens and keys of the inventory. The owner states no interval and no rotation event for any class of the inventory. A rotation at a fixed cadence would bind a manual swap of keys across machines that no easy mechanism serves, so the rotation of a single secret stays a manual step that its own cause starts. Alternatives that lost: a cadence rule that names an interval or a rotation event for each class of the inventory, option a of the park comment of issue 373, because the cost of the manual swap outweighs the benefit of the cadence while no easier mechanism for the swap exists. Consequences: the Security Law of docs/secrets.md gains one item that cites this record, so an audit finding that names only the age of a credential closes against this record. The oldest credentials of the inventory stay unchanged until a cause of their own starts a rotation or the reopen trigger fires. The rotation of any single secret stays outside the scope of issue 373. Status: accepted. Reopens: an easier mechanism for swapping keys across machines is introduced.
